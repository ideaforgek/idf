IdeaForge is a focused, single-event hackathon for curious minds ready to turn a real-world problem into a working idea. Form a team of 2–4, bring your perspective, and make the time count.

02 RULES OF THE FORGE

Fair play.
Bold thinking.

Keep these in mind so every team gets a fair shot.

01

Build your crew

Each team must have 2–4 members. A participant may belong to one team only.

02

Make it yours

Submit original work. Credit third-party code, datasets, APIs, and other resources you use.

03

Use tools responsibly

AI tools and public resources are welcome unless announced otherwise. Your team must understand and explain its work.

04

Show what you built

Be ready to demo your solution and explain the problem, your approach, and each member’s contribution.

05

Respect the room

No plagiarism, harassment, unauthorized access, or disruption. Keep collaboration respectful and safe.

06

Play fair

Follow organizer instructions and submit within the announced timelines. Organizer decisions are final.

Event schedule, judging criteria, and any event-specific requirements will be shared by the organizers.
